A collectible piece of architecture designed for two people to celebrate, reconnect and disappear for a while.

Cabin SOL was designed by Mexico City’s oioioi Studio and brought to Texas as the first O-Frame cabin in the United States. Its curved door opens the entire cabin to the pines. A circular window frames the sunrise from bed.

Before you book

Good to know.

How private is Cabin SOL?

SOL’s cabin, deck, hot tub, sauna, outdoor shower and fire pit are private and never shared. The Bali Dome sits elsewhere on the 15-acre property, with each stay designed to feel separate.

How far is it from Dallas?

Cabin SOL is on private acreage outside Paris, Texas, approximately 90 minutes northeast of Dallas. A personal vehicle is required.

Who is the cabin designed for?

Cabin SOL accommodates a maximum of two adults in one queen bed. It is designed as a couples retreat and is not suitable for children.

Are one-night stays available?

One-night stays may be available on select weeknights. Weekend and holiday minimums vary by date.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes. Up to two dogs are allowed with a $49 fee per dog. Cats and other pets are not permitted.

What is included?

The cabin includes a queen bed, full private bathroom, compact integrated kitchen with an indoor induction cooktop, Starlink Wi-Fi, climate control, cedar hot tub, private sauna, outdoor shower, Weber grill and gas fire pit.

Is there a full bathroom inside?

Yes. SOL has a full private indoor bathroom. The outdoor rainfall shower is an additional amenity.

Is parking and self-check-in available?

Yes. On-site parking and self-check-in are available. Arrival instructions and the exact address are shared with registered guests before check-in.

Is the cabin easy to access?

SOL is reached by an elevated cedar walkway and has steps to the deck. It may not be suitable for guests with mobility limitations.

What are check-in and checkout times?

Standard check-in is 3:00 PM and checkout is 10:00 AM. Early check-in or a 12:00 PM late checkout may be available for $40, depending on availability.
