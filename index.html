Before you book

Good to know.

How private is Cabin SOL?

The cabin and its outdoor amenities sit on 15 private acres. The hot tub, sauna, outdoor shower, deck and fire pit are not shared with other guests.

How far is it from Dallas?

The property is roughly 90 minutes northeast of Dallas near Paris, Texas. A personal vehicle is required.

Who is the cabin designed for?

Cabin SOL accommodates a maximum of two guests in one queen bed. It is designed as a couples retreat and is not child-friendly.

What is included?

The cabin includes a full private bathroom, compact integrated kitchen, Starlink WiFi, climate control, cedar hot tub, private sauna, outdoor shower, BBQ grill and fire pit.

Are pets allowed?

One well-behaved dog may be approved. A pet fee applies and pets may not be left unattended in the cabin.

What should we expect from the rural setting?

This is a countryside stay in East Texas. Wildlife, insects, changing weather and gravel access are part of the setting. The cabin is fully climate controlled and the property is regularly treated.

What are check-in and checkout times?

Standard check-in is 3:00 PM and checkout is 10:00 AM. Any approved exceptions will be confirmed before arrival.
